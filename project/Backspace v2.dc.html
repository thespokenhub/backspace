HOW IT WORKS

Good questions, short answers.

Where do old pages come from?
Since 1996, websites have been saved again and again, like photos taken of a page over the years. Backspace finds every photo of the address you type.
Is this the real page?
Yes. It's the page's actual text and code from that day. Some images or videos may be missing if they weren't saved at the time.
Why are some dates missing?
Pages aren't saved every day. Popular sites have copies almost weekly. Small sites might have one a year, or none.
